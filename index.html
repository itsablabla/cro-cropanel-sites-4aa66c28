Said before buying — these are conversion problems

Misleading product information
Misleading information: the customer feels they were sold something different from what they got.
— wisernotify.com
Prospects are confused or feel misled when the product or service description doesn't match the reality, leading to unmet expectations.
Hidden or unexpected fees
The user is enticed with a low advertised price. After investing time and effort, they discover unexpected fees and charges when they reach the checkout.
— deceptive.design
Prospects feel deceived when the initial advertised price is low but additional mandatory charges are revealed late in the purchase process, making price comparison difficult and increasing perceived risk.
Confusing pricing structures
Other ways some businesses tend to overcomplicate pricing include hidden fees, inconsistent pricing across channels, frequent price changes, convoluted bundling, and confusing loyalty programs.
— netsuite.com
Prospects are confounded by complex pricing models, inconsistent prices, or unclear loyalty programs, making it hard to understand the true cost or value.
Unclear return and refund policies
If your refund policy is unclear, it can harm customer experience and make your roadmap for processing requests difficult.
— squarespace.com
Prospects feel risky or confused when return and refund policies are not clearly communicated upfront, impacting their confidence in making a purchase.
Lack of transparency on past issues
If you see a negative review while considering a new product, you’d probably want to know how the seller handled that previous experience.
— ever-help.com
Prospects feel uncertain about buying if they cannot see how a company addresses past customer complaints, leading to concerns about future support or product reliability.
